FAQ

Radio, Explained

How do I play songs?

Put your authorized audio files inside the audio folder using the filenames specified in script.js. You can also click "Choose Audio" to load your own audio files directly.

Why aren't commercial songs included?

Commercial Bollywood recordings are copyrighted. This template therefore does not distribute unauthorized MP3 files. Use audio that you own, have licensed, or otherwise have permission to play.

Does the player work on mobile?

Yes. The interface is responsive and uses the browser's native audio playback engine.

Can I add more artists?

Yes. Add another playlist array in script.js and connect it to another playlist tab.
